Privacy Policy: RangeDot

Last updated: 2026-11-19

RangeDot ("the app") is published by SIA "ZeroLN", registration No. 40203796385, registered office 5 Meldru iela, Sigulda, Siguldas novads, LV-2150, Latvia ("we"). This policy covers the app on Android and iOS. We are the data controller for the limited data described under "Contacting support".

Summary

ZeroLN does not collect any data through the app. We receive data only when you send it to us yourself, for example in a Support email (see "Sharing" and "Contacting support"). We do not sell any data. The app has no accounts and no advertising, and the app's own code makes no internet connection.

The purchase of the full version goes through the app store. On Android, the purchase function of the app is the Google Play Billing Library, which Google supplies, and the Google Privacy Policy applies to it. On iOS, the purchase function of the app is StoreKit, which Apple supplies as a part of iOS, and the Apple Privacy Policy applies to it. See "Purchase of the full version".

What the app uses on your device

  • Camera: to watch your paper target and find new bullet holes. Images are analysed on your phone.
  • Microphone (optional): to hear the bang of each shot, count shots and check the target. The sound level is analysed live. The sound is saved only as part of a session video (see Storage), and only while you watch a target.
  • Debug files: each saved session also saves a video of the screen view (the analysed camera image with the detection area, the marks and the status, about 10 frames a second, without sound), a log of app events, and small image crops (squares of 64 pixels) of spots that the app checked. The log holds the camera settings (for example exposure and zoom) and how much the phone moved around each bang (from the motion sensors). These files help us to find detection problems. The app keeps them for the latest saved session only: when a new saved session starts, it deletes the debug files of the older sessions. They are also deleted with the session. They stay in the app's own storage on your phone. They leave the phone only when you attach them to a Support email (see "Sharing").
  • Storage: each session (a reference image, one image per shot, a video of about 1 second of each shot without sound, a small file with shot positions and app settings, and, when "Save session videos" is on (it is on by default; you can turn it off in Settings), a video of the analysed camera image with the microphone sound, from Start to Finish) is saved inside the app's own storage on your phone. While the app watches, it records the camera image for these short videos, and it deletes the longer recording as soon as the short videos are cut. On iOS, sessions are visible in the Files app under the app's folder.
  • Your photos and videos: the app does not open or show the photos and videos of your phone.
  • Photos / Gallery: only when you tap Save, the session images are added to your photo library. On iOS, the app asks only for permission to *add* photos, never to read them.

Purchase of the full version

The app runs as a free trial for 7 days from the first time you press Start. After the free trial, Start needs the full version: one purchase, no subscription. The purchase goes through Google Play (Android) or the App Store (iOS). The store takes the payment and keeps the purchase record under the store's own privacy policy. We do not receive your payment details. For the purchase, the app stores on your phone whether the full version is owned, when the free trial started and the latest clock time that it saw. To check the purchase, the app asks the store at each start (on Android also each time that you return to the main screen or to Settings). The saved sessions, the settings and the help stay available without the purchase.

On Android, the purchase function of the app is the Google Play Billing Library, which Google supplies. The Google Privacy Policy applies to this library and to any data that Google collects through it. ZeroLN does not collect or receive these data.

On iOS, the purchase function of the app is StoreKit, which Apple supplies as a part of iOS. The Apple Privacy Policy applies to StoreKit and to any data that Apple collects through it. ZeroLN does not collect or receive these data. The app adds no other library for the purchase.

Sharing

Apart from the purchase function (see "Purchase of the full version") and the backup of your phone (see "Your control"), the app sends nothing by itself. Three actions can send data, and only when you choose them:

  • Share session sends the images, the short shot videos and the session video of that session to an app that you pick in the share sheet of your phone, for example WhatsApp, Messages or your email app. It sends no debug files.
  • Support (in the same share sheet) opens a form. When you press "Write the email", the app packs the files of that session into one zip file: the images, the short shot videos, the session video and the file with shot positions. If the session has debug files and you leave "Attach the debug files" on, the zip file also holds the screen video, the log and the image crops (see "Debug files"). The session video holds the microphone sound. The email also contains your text, the app version, the phone model and the OS version. The videos and the audio can show people and voices near the camera; the form tells you this and the size of the files. Your email app then opens with the email: you can read and change it, and you decide whether to send it. The app keeps its copy of the zip file only until the next zip or the next start of the app.
  • Support in Settings > About writes an email to us with the app version, the phone model and the OS version. When your latest session has debug files, the app first asks whether to attach them as one zip file: the screen video, the log, the image crops and the file with the shot positions and the app settings. The email then also names the session (date and time) and the zip file. You can decline; the email then has no attachment. The screen video and the crops can show people near the target. The app keeps its copy of the zip file only until the next zip or the next start of the app.

If you send a report to us, we use it only to fix the problem you reported, and we delete it within 90 days after the fix is released, unless you agree that we keep it as a test case.

Contacting support

If you email us, we process your email address, your message and any attachments you choose to send. We use them only to answer you and fix the problem. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in providing support (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)). We keep support emails for up to 12 months after the case is closed, and attachments as described under "Sharing". We do not share them with anyone except our email provider, Google Ireland Limited (Google Workspace), which processes them on our behalf.

Your rights (GDPR)

You can ask us for access to, correction of, or deletion of the personal data we hold about you from support contacts, and you can object to its processing. Write to support@zeroln.com. For the purchase functions, see the Google Privacy Policy (Android) and the Apple Privacy Policy (iOS). You can also complain to the Latvian Data State Inspectorate (Datu valsts inspekcija, www.dvi.gov.lv) or to the data protection authority in your own country.

Your control

You can delete any session in the app. Uninstalling the app removes all its data from your phone. Images you saved to your photo library stay there until you delete them. On Android, the backup of your phone to your Google account and a transfer to a new phone can include the app settings, the purchase state and the dates of the free trial. The sessions stay out of the backup and the transfer. Google keeps the backup for you; we have no access to it. On iOS, the backup of your iPhone to iCloud or to a computer, when it is on, can include the app data. The Apple Privacy Policy applies to iCloud. We have no access to these backups.

Children

The app is made for target shooters of 13 years and older. It is not directed at children under 13. ZeroLN does not collect any data through the app, from children or from anyone else. For the purchase functions of Google (Android) and Apple (iOS), see "Purchase of the full version".
